About TechNova 2025

TechNova is not just another college fest – it’s a celebration of technology, creativity, and innovation. From thought-provoking talks to hands-on challenges, we aim to inspire and engage every participant.

Our Story

What started as a small gathering of tech enthusiasts has now grown into a platform where ideas, innovation, and inspiration come together. Over the years, TechNova has become a stage for young innovators to showcase their skills and for students to connect with industry experts.

Our Mission

To empower students with opportunities to learn, build, and share their ideas in technology.

Our Vision

To create a vibrant ecosystem where creativity meets technology, shaping tomorrow’s innovators.
